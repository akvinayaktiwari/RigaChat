An AI sales agent is software that talks to prospects for you: it answers questions, asks qualifying questions, captures contact details and follows up. It does not negotiate, close a complicated deal, or know anything you never told it. The useful question is not whether it can replace a salesperson but which part of the first conversation it can take off one.

## What can an AI sales agent do well?

The work that is repetitive, immediate and rule-shaped.

- **Answer common questions at any hour** from your own content: what you offer, how it works, where you operate, how to book.
- **Ask qualifying questions** in a natural order, such as what the person wants, when, and roughly what budget.
- **Capture contact details** before the visitor leaves.
- **Follow up** with a message, wait for a reply, and nudge once if the person goes quiet.
- **Hand over** to a person with the conversation attached.

Speed is the point. A prospect who asks at midnight gets an answer at midnight, and your team starts the morning with a qualified list instead of a blank inbox.

## What can't it do?

- **Negotiate.** Price, terms and exceptions need a person with the authority to agree.
- **Know what it was not given.** It answers from your content. A question outside it should get an honest "I don't know" and an offer of a person.
- **Close a complex or high-value sale.** At best it qualifies and books the conversation.
- **Read a room.** It can miss frustration or a hint that a person would pick up.
- **Replace follow-through.** A hot lead the team ignores is still lost.

<Callout title="The test that matters" tone="warning">
  Ask it something your content does not cover. A good agent says it does not know and offers a person. A bad one answers confidently and wrongly. Do this before you put it in front of customers.
</Callout>

## Where should a person step in?

Plan the handoff before launch. The agent should pass the prospect to a person when they ask for one, when they are ready to decide, when they are unhappy, or when the question is outside what it was given. The person should receive the conversation, not a name and number, and be alerted quickly.

## How do you judge one before buying?

Five questions, whichever product you are looking at.

1. **What does it answer from?** Your own content, or a general model that may guess.
2. **What happens when it does not know?** It should say so and offer a person.
3. **What does it do after the first conversation?** Follow-up is where leads are won back.
4. **How does my team get the lead?** In what form, how fast, with what attached.
5. **Which channels does it actually cover?** Chat, voice on a web page and phone calls are different products. Ask for the exact one. We cover the voice split in [AI receptionist vs website voice agent](/blog/ai-receptionist-vs-website-voice-agent/).
