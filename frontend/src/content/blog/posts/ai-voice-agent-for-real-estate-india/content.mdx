An AI voice agent for real estate talks to a prospective buyer on your project page and answers routine questions out loud from your knowledge base: where the project is, which configurations exist, what the amenities are, and how to book a site visit. When the buyer shows real intent, or asks something the agent cannot confirm, it hands over to your sales team instead of improvising.

Most of the work is not in the agent's voice. It is in deciding which questions it may answer, because a property enquiry mixes questions that are safe to answer automatically with ones that can cost you a sale, or a complaint, if answered wrongly. This post sorts them.

## Which questions can a real estate voice agent answer well?

Questions whose answer is stable, factual and already on your project materials. If the answer would be the same for every buyer and would not change next week, the agent can give it with confidence.

- **Location and connectivity.** Where the project is, what is nearby, how to reach it.
- **Configurations and layouts.** Which unit types exist and their general descriptions.
- **Amenities.** What the project offers, as listed in the brochure.
- **The process.** How to book a site visit, what to bring, what the next step is.
- **About the developer.** Who built it and what else they have delivered, as far as you have written it down.

These are also the questions a visitor asks first, which is the point: answering them immediately keeps the visitor on the page until a human can take over.

## Which questions should it hand to your sales team?

Anything that depends on the specific buyer or the specific unit, and anything that involves a commitment. Handing these over is not a failure of the agent; it is the design.

| Question | What the agent should do |
| --- | --- |
| Final price, discounts, offers | Share only an indicative range you have written down, then hand off |
| Payment plans, loans, taxes | Hand off to someone who can discuss the buyer's situation |
| Is a particular unit available | Answer only from a current knowledge base, otherwise say it will confirm with the team |
| Possession date | State only a date you have confirmed in writing; otherwise hand off |
| Negotiation, complaints | Hand off immediately |
| "Can I speak to someone?" | Hand off immediately, every time |

The [Vyostra AI voice agent](/features/voice-agent/) has a handoff action for this: when a visitor asks for a person, is frustrated, or raises something needing a decision, it can alert your team. The agent decides from the meaning of what was said rather than from a fixed list of phrases, since people ask for a human in endless ways.

## Why are availability and price the risky questions?

Because the agent states them with the same confidence whether they are right or wrong, and a buyer remembers a spoken number. A chat message can be re-read and doubted. A confident voice saying a unit is available, or giving a price lower than the real one, creates an expectation your sales team then has to undo in front of the customer.

Treat both as data-freshness problems, not agent problems. If someone updates the knowledge base the moment a unit is sold or a price changes, the agent can answer. If nobody does, the safest instruction you can give is: "do not confirm availability or final price; offer to have the sales team confirm."

## What should the agent say about RERA?

Nothing beyond verified text you have given it. A spoken claim about registration status is the kind of statement a buyer relies on, so it must match exactly what is registered. The rules on how projects are advertised, and what registration details must appear, are set under RERA and differ by state authority, so check what your own state authority requires for marketing and give the agent only that verified registration information to repeat.

<Callout title="A rule worth writing into the agent's instructions" tone="warning">
If the knowledge base does not contain the answer, the agent should say so and offer a person. A voice agent that guesses a regulatory fact, a price or a possession date is more dangerous than no agent, because it sounds sure.
</Callout>

## How should the handoff work in a property business?

It should reach a person who is actually available and who already knows what the buyer asked. A site-visit enquiry that waits a day for a callback is the failure the agent was installed to prevent, so be honest about your hours. If your team works until a certain time, the agent should say when someone will be in touch, not promise an immediate call it cannot guarantee.

Once you have the buyer's number, the follow-up belongs on WhatsApp, where you can send the brochure, confirm the visit and nudge politely. The rules that govern that channel are in [the WhatsApp guide for real estate in India](/blog/whatsapp-chatbot-for-real-estate-india/).

## How do you roll this out without embarrassing yourself?

Start narrow and widen as it proves itself.

1. **Write a small, accurate knowledge base** for one project: location, configurations, amenities, site-visit process.
2. **Tell the agent what not to answer**: price, availability and possession unless confirmed.
3. **Test with real questions**, including the awkward ones, spoken in the way your buyers speak. The [on-page voice agent guide](/blog/ai-voice-agent-on-your-website-india/) has a checklist.
4. **Staff the handoff** before you announce it.
5. **Widen the knowledge base** only once the first version answers correctly.
