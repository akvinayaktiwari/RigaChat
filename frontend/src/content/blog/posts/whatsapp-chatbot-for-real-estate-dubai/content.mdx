A WhatsApp chatbot for a Dubai brokerage is an automated agent on a WhatsApp Business number. It answers a buyer's enquiry as soon as it arrives, asks the few questions a viewing depends on, records the answers on the lead, and either proposes a viewing or hands the chat to an agent with the conversation attached.

It works only inside WhatsApp's rules and Dubai's advertising and data rules, and it cannot capture enquiries that never reach WhatsApp. This guide covers what the chatbot should do, which Dubai details change the design, and where it stops.

> The competitor is not another chatbot. It is the agent who answers in two minutes, and the enquiry that waited until morning.

## Why does a Dubai brokerage put the chatbot on WhatsApp?

Because the conversation tends to move there. A buyer finds a listing, a launch ad or your website, and then wants to carry on in the app they already use, with a thread they can scroll back through when they compare projects. Property Finder, for one, [lets a seeker contact an agent on WhatsApp](https://www.propertyfinder.ae/blog/enquire-about-a-property-with-an-agent-via-whatsapp/) as well as by email or phone.

That also marks the limit. A WhatsApp chatbot sees the enquiries that arrive on WhatsApp. Email and phone enquiries from the same listing do not pass through it, so check how each portal you pay for delivers its leads before you plan around a bot. The wider question of getting every source into one list is covered in [real estate CRM for Dubai brokers](/blog/real-estate-crm-dubai/).

## What rules limit what the chatbot may send?

Three sets, and they apply together.

Property Finder's [guidance for agents](https://www.propertyfinder.ae/partnerhub/whatsapp-for-agents-part-1-compliant-opt-in-funnels-that-convert/) adds that UAE telecom rules expect prior consent and a working opt-out for marketing messages. It attributes that to the telecom regulator; read the regulator's own text before relying on it, and ask your legal adviser what applies to your brokerage.

## What does a good Dubai property chat look like?

Short. It qualifies, then gets out of the way. Five moves, in this order.

    { phase: 'Qualify', timeline: '3 to 5 questions', action: 'Budget, preferred area, ready or off-plan, when they want to move, and cash or mortgage. Ask nothing you will not act on.' },

Three Dubai details change the wording of that flow:

- **Off-plan and ready are different conversations.** A launch enquiry is about payment plan and handover; a resale enquiry is about the unit, the tenancy and the mortgage. Asking "ready or off-plan?" early lets the bot choose the right questions.
- **Buyers write from many countries.** Do not assume a phone number or a language. Capture numbers in international format, with the country code, at the point of entry, so WhatsApp can deliver to them.
- **Never let the bot write property claims.** Price, availability, ROI or "guaranteed" anything should come only from content you have approved, with the permit details your ad already carries. A bot that improvises about a unit is an advertising problem, not a technical one.

## How does the lead source change the first message?

The source decides whether the 24-hour window is already open.

| Source | Who messages first | Your first message |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Click-to-WhatsApp ad | The buyer, from the ad | Free-form. Reply fast and qualify. |
| Chat on your own website | The buyer, on your site | Depends on the opt-in you captured. Say plainly that you will continue on WhatsApp. |
| Meta lead ad form | Nobody; you receive a form | An approved template, sent within seconds of the submission. |
| Portal enquiry on WhatsApp | The buyer, on the portal's WhatsApp link | Check how the portal passes it to you. Do not assume it reaches your bot. |
| Email or phone enquiry | Not WhatsApp at all | The chatbot is not involved. |

[Click-to-WhatsApp ads versus lead forms](/blog/click-to-whatsapp-ads-vs-lead-forms-real-estate-india/) compares the two ad paths, and [auto-replying to Facebook lead ads](/blog/facebook-lead-ads-whatsapp-auto-reply/) covers the template path.

## What does it cost to run?

Two things: the chatbot platform, and Meta's charge for template messages. Meta's [pricing page](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing) says that since 1 July 2025 you are charged per delivered template message, at a rate that depends on the template category and on the recipient's country calling code. It also says:

- replies inside an open 24-hour window are free;
- a utility template sent inside that window is free;
- when a buyer messages you through a click-to-WhatsApp ad, a reply within 24 hours opens a 72-hour window in which every message, templates included, is free.

Meta changes rates and rules, so read the current rate card before you budget. No figure is quoted here for that reason.
