A click-to-WhatsApp ad and a lead form ad both run on Facebook and Instagram, but they start completely different things. A **click-to-WhatsApp ad** opens a WhatsApp chat with your business, so the buyer sends the first message and the conversation is open from the start. A **lead form ad** opens an instant form that collects answers like name, phone and budget, and nobody is talking yet: you have to start the WhatsApp conversation yourself, with an approved template.

For a property developer or broker in India, that difference decides who your sales team talks to first, how fast, and what WhatsApp lets you send.

## How do the two ad types compare?

| | Click-to-WhatsApp ad | Lead form ad |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What the buyer does | Taps the ad and lands in a WhatsApp chat with you | Fills in an instant form without leaving Facebook or Instagram |
| Who sends the first message | The buyer | You, after the form is submitted |
| What your first message can be | Any reply, within 24 hours of the buyer's last message | An approved template only |
| What you capture | Whatever the buyer types, plus their WhatsApp number | Structured answers to the questions you set, and the phone number they enter |
| How the lead reaches your team | As a WhatsApp conversation on your business number | Through Meta's lead webhook to your CRM, or a CSV export |
| What it needs | A Facebook Page with a WhatsApp number linked | A Facebook Page and an instant form |

Sources: Meta's pages on [ads that click to WhatsApp](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/ad-creative/messaging-ads/click-to-whatsapp), the [lead webhook](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/graph-api/webhooks/getting-started/webhooks-for-leadgen/) and [sending messages](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/messages/send-messages).

## What does a click-to-WhatsApp ad actually do?

Meta describes it plainly: these ads "send people who click on your ads directly into conversations with your business in WhatsApp." They run on [Facebook and Instagram, in feeds, Stories and Marketplace](https://whatsappbusiness.com/products/ads-that-click-to-whatsapp/), and the chat opens with a pre-filled message the buyer can send or edit.

Because the buyer sends that first message, two WhatsApp rules work in your favour:

The catch is the other side of the same coin: the buyer is now waiting in a live chat. A click-to-WhatsApp campaign that sends enquiries to a number nobody watches after 7pm turns ad spend into unanswered messages.

## What does a lead form ad do better?

It asks everyone the same questions. For a launch, that matters more than speed of chat: the sales team calls with budget, configuration, preferred location and possession timeline already in hand, and the leads can be sorted before anyone dials.

A lead form also delivers the lead to software the moment it is submitted. Meta sends a [webhook notification](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/graph-api/webhooks/getting-started/webhooks-for-leadgen/) with a lead ID, and your system fetches the answers through the Graph API. From there the lead can go straight into your CRM and, if the buyer opted in, receive a WhatsApp template within seconds. The full chain is in [how to auto-reply on WhatsApp to Facebook lead ads](/blog/facebook-lead-ads-whatsapp-auto-reply).

<Callout title="Opt-in belongs on the form" tone="warning">
  A lead form only lets you message someone on WhatsApp if they agreed to it. Meta's [opt-in policy](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/getting-opt-in) asks that the person is clearly told they are opting in to messages and the name of the business sending them. Put that line on the form. A phone number on its own is not permission.
</Callout>

## Which should a property developer in India choose?

Choose by what your team can do with the lead, not by which ad looks cheaper in Ads Manager.

## What does RERA require in these ads?

The ad type does not change your RERA duties. [Section 11(2) of the RERA Act](https://ibclaw.in/section-11-of-real-estate-regulation-and-development-act-2016-rera-functions-and-duties-of-promoter/) says an advertisement or prospectus issued by a promoter "shall mention prominently the website address of the Authority" and "include the registration number obtained from the Authority." That applies to the creative of a click-to-WhatsApp ad and a lead form ad alike. Several state authorities have issued their own directions on how prominently to show it, so check your state RERA's circulars. This is a summary of the Act, not legal advice.
