### Long description

Vyostra AI is a lead-capture platform for businesses that sell through enquiries, such as real estate brokerages and developers.

You train an AI agent on your website and your own knowledge base. It answers visitors on website chat at any hour, and an optional voice agent lets them talk to your site in the browser, with no phone number. The agent answers only from the content you give it. When the answer is not there, it says so instead of guessing.

When a conversation turns into interest, the agent collects the lead's details and writes them into a built-in lead CRM, so every enquiry sits in one queue with its transcript. Leads from embeddable lead forms and Meta lead ads arrive in the same queue. Your team gets a WhatsApp alert for each new lead, and follow-up journeys continue the conversation on WhatsApp, waiting for a real reply rather than firing on a timer, then hand the lead to a person.

Form and Meta lead ad leads can be created in Zoho CRM automatically. A REST API with scoped keys lets developers read leads, chatbots, forms and voice agents, and create lead forms.

Plans start at $49 a month with a 14-day free trial.

### Feature list

- AI chat agent trained on your website and knowledge base
- Answers only from your content; says so when it does not know
- One script tag to embed
- In-browser voice agent (add-on, no phone number needed)
- Built-in lead CRM with the full transcript on every lead
- Embeddable lead form builder
- Meta lead ads integration (Facebook and Instagram lead forms)
- WhatsApp alerts for each new lead, and a weekly report
- WhatsApp follow-up journeys that wait for a reply
- Zoho CRM sync for form and Meta lead ad leads
- REST API and API keys with permission scopes

### Who it is for

Small and mid-sized businesses that sell through enquiries (real estate brokerages and developers first), and agencies running lead capture for several clients.
