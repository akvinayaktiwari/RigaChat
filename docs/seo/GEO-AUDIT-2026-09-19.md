# vyostra.com — GEO / AEO Audit

**Audited:** 2026-09-19, `main` at `d27a806`
**Lens:** the checklist from `QDNCO-SEO-AEO-AUDIT.md` and the measurement gaps in
`prompt-analytics.md`, applied to Vyostra's marketing site.
**Method:** `npm run build` with the production origin, followed by static analysis of all
15 prerendered pages in `frontend/dist/`. Checks covered sitemap parity, canonicals,
titles, descriptions, H1s, OG tags, JSON-LD parsing, images, the internal link graph,
and raw-HTML text. Live probes of `vyostra.com` confirmed that the deployed
`robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` are byte-identical to `main`.
**Not covered:** Search Console, GA4, CrUX field data, backlinks, and off-site brand
mentions.

## llms.txt Status

**Missing** (`/llms.txt` returns 404). qdnco.com treats its `llms.txt` as a key asset.
Google stated on 2026-05-15 that `llms.txt` does not affect Search or AI Overviews.
Server-log studies also show major AI crawlers rarely fetch it. It is worth 30 minutes
as a clean fact sheet for non-Google systems, and it is not a ranking lever.

If you add one, generate it in `crawl-files.ts` next to robots and sitemap so it cannot
drift from the route list. Contents: what Vyostra is (one definition sentence), the 3
USD plans ($49 / $129 / $349), channels (chat, voice, WhatsApp), the built-in CRM,
Bangalore, support@, and one line per prerendered page.

## Passage-Level Citability

**What already works (keep doing this):**

- The WhatsApp post opens with a textbook definition sentence: *"A WhatsApp chatbot for
  real estate is an automated agent on a WhatsApp Business number that answers a
  property enquiry the moment it lands…"* That is exactly the "X is…" pattern,
  inside the first 60 words.
- `## Build, buy, or the free app: an honest comparison` is the same self-limiting
  framing the qdnco audit credits for its AEO results.
- 6 FAQ answers at 60–80 words each are self-contained and extractable.
- The source comment deliberately avoids invented performance figures. That is correct.

**What's missing:**

1. **Zero outbound links in either post.** The WhatsApp post explains Meta's 24-hour
   window, templates, opt-in and pricing. Its own header comment says it
   *"points at the current docs instead of a number"*, but it contains no link to
   those docs. The pilgrimage post labels blocks "Sourced data" and a "Note on the
   footfall numbers" with **no URLs at all**. An unlinked "sourced" claim is the
   weakest possible version of a citation.
2. **No block in the 134–167-word range.** The FAQ answers are about half that. Each
   post's top section should have one fuller self-contained answer block.
3. **The pilgrimage post is off-topic.** A hospitality feasibility study sits on an AI
   chatbot/CRM domain and dilutes the topical signal. Fine as a lead magnet for
   real-estate developers, but frame it that way ("how developers use lead capture to
   pre-sell a project like this") or accept it earns nothing for "AI chatbot" queries.

## Server-Side Rendering Check

✅ **All 15 indexable URLs are prerendered** (`scripts/prerender.mjs` +
`prerender-entry.tsx`). Raw HTML text ranges from 722 characters (`/contact/`) to
17,741 (the pilgrimage post). The homepage carries 6,838 characters and 37 H2/H3s.
CloudFront 301s bare paths to their trailing-slash form. It serves `app-shell.html`,
never the homepage, to client routes. Missing files return real 404s.

⚠ **JS weight (a CWV issue, not an indexing one):** every marketing page loads
`index-*.js` at **1,069 KB (283 KB gzip)**. Only `BlogIndex` and `BlogPost` are
`lazy()`. The other ~50 page imports in `App.tsx`, including the whole dashboard, journey
builder, and voice agent, ship to a first-time visitor on `/`. This is qdnco's #2
finding in milder form: it does not hurt crawling, but it costs LCP and INP on Indian
mid-range Android devices.

## Schema Recommendations

- **`BreadcrumbList`** on feature pages and blog posts. qdnco ships 96. Vyostra ships
  none.
- **Feature pages** (`/features/*`) have **no JSON-LD**. Give each a `WebPage` with
  `about` pointing to the `SoftwareApplication` `@id`, plus a breadcrumb.
- **`SoftwareApplication`**: add `@id` so feature pages can reference it. Add
  `featureList`. Add INR `Offer`s alongside USD, because UPI plans exist and India is a
  target market. Add `aggregateRating` only once real reviews exist, never invented.
- **`BlogPosting`**: set `author` as a Person, add `dateModified`, and use a per-post
  `image` instead of the site-wide `og-image.png`.
- **`Organization`**: add `contactPoint` (support@, `areaServed: ["IN","AE"]`) and
  grow `sameAs` as profiles go live.
- Keep both `FAQPage` blocks. They earn no Google rich results (restricted since 2023),
  but they are clean Q/A pairs for LLM parsing.

## Content Reformatting Suggestions

1. **WhatsApp post H2s → questions.** Match real queries:
   - `## The four rules that decide your design` → `## What rules does WhatsApp enforce on business messages?`
   - `## Build, buy, or the free app: an honest comparison` → `## Should you build, buy, or use the free WhatsApp Business app?`
   - `## Before you switch it on` → `## What do you need before launching a WhatsApp chatbot?`
2. **Title and description trims:**
   - WhatsApp post title, 75 characters → `WhatsApp Chatbot for Real Estate in India — Vyostra AI` (54).
   - Its description is **299 characters**. Cut it to ~155 and lead with the definition.
   - Homepage title (69), and the descriptions for home (180), about (175),
     pilgrimage (178) and help (171) all truncate.
3. **Help page:** the visible heading is "Still need help?", and the 13 questions render
   as buttons. Group them under 3–4 question-phrased H2s ("Setting up your agent",
   "WhatsApp", "Billing").
4. **Homepage:** add one 134–167-word "What is Vyostra AI?" block high on the page,
   pairing the definition with channels, CRM, pricing floor and markets. This is the
   passage an engine should lift when asked "what is Vyostra".
5. **Add at least one real visual per post.** A WhatsApp flow diagram and a
   24-hour-window timeline would each double as a per-post OG image and close the
   multi-modal gap.

## Not in the original audit: the claims were false

Production was checked before touching the copy. It held **181 leads** (38 chat,
26 form, 117 Meta), **16 client records**, 1,429 conversations and 2 payment
rows. The homepage claimed *50,000+ leads captured*, *500+ businesses live* and
a *94% resolution rate* — a metric nothing in the product computes. The hero
also showed five review stars over invented customer avatars, while
`structured-data.ts` deliberately publishes no `aggregateRating` because no
reviews exist.

All of it was replaced with facts true by construction: 3 channels, 24/7, $49
from `PRICING_TIERS`, 1 inbox. The testimonials were left alone — that file
records them as real customers confirmed on 2026-09-15.

This mattered more than an ordinary copy fix: the pages were reworked to be
quoted by answer engines, and an invented number on a quotable page is an
invented number that gets repeated.

## Where the score stands

The 63/100 above was not re-scored formally. Citability, structure and
technical accessibility all improved and are now the site's strong side. The
two that did not move are **multi-modal** (still zero images on all 15 pages)
and **authority** (posts are still authored by the organisation, `sameAs` still
lists one profile). Both are blocked on inputs, not on engineering.

**The real constraint is unchanged: two posts.** No remaining on-page fix beats
publishing more.
